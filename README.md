# Book_flow

**A Bookkeeping and Financial Record Management System**

## 1. Overview

Book_flow is a full-stack bookkeeping and financial record management application being developed to help a small gardening services business organise and manage its financial activities.

The application aims to bring important financial records into one centralised system, making it easier to manage invoices, record income and expenses, monitor business finances and prepare organised financial information for accounting purposes.

This project is being developed as both a practical solution to a real business problem and an opportunity to strengthen my software development skills.

## 2. Problem Statement

The gardening services business currently faces challenges with organising and managing its financial records.

Financial information is scattered across different places, making it difficult to maintain organised records and track the business's financial position.

Some of the challenges include:

- Disorganised invoices and financial documents.
- Difficulty keeping track of business income and expenses.
- Limited visibility into how much money the business receives and spends.
- Time spent organising financial records before they can be reviewed by an accountant.
- High accounting costs associated with the preparation and organisation of financial information.

These challenges highlight the need for a centralised system that can simplify bookkeeping processes and improve financial record management.

## 3. Project Purpose

The purpose of Book_flow is to develop a system that helps the business:

- Keep financial records organised in one place.
- Create and manage invoices.
- Record and track business income.
- Record and categorise business expenses.
- Monitor financial transactions.
- Generate useful financial summaries and reports.
- Prepare organised financial records for the accountant.

The goal is to improve the business's bookkeeping processes, reduce unnecessary administrative work and make financial information easier to access and understand.

## 5. Planned Features

### Invoice Management

- Create invoices.
- Capture customer information.
- Add services and prices.
- Calculate invoice totals.
- Track paid and unpaid invoices.
- Generate PDF invoices.

### Income Management

- Record payments received.
- Link payments to invoices.
- Track business income.
- View income history.

### Expense Management

- Record business expenses.
- Categorise expenses.
- Capture expense dates and descriptions.
- Keep track of business spending.

### Financial Tracking

- View income and expenses.
- Calculate income minus expenses.
- View financial summaries.
- Generate reports for record-keeping and accounting purposes.

### Customer Management

- Add and manage customer details.
- View customer information.
- Access customer invoice history.
